# Refresh refreshes everything, in every section
Topics: [[mail-window]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[pop3-smtp-sending]]

(the owner) **Refresh is Send and Receive wherever it is pressed**: the Outbox, the mail of every account included, and the calendars, task lists and address books, also from Calendar, Contacts or Tasks. **The Operations window shows exactly what runs**: every account whose calendars synchronise has its own row, "<Shown as> (Calendars and contacts)", with Done or its error (also in the Errors tab), under the same three ticks; a calendar error is no longer a red status line. A Refresh pressed while one runs is ignored; the busy bar shows that something runs. Why: "Refresh should refresh everything. It does not depend on what part of the application I happen to be viewing."
