# A stored password shows as 16 dots; the eye only while editing
Topics: [[security]] · [[look]]

(the owner) **A password or secret that is stored shows in its field as 16 dots, whatever its length, and an empty field means nothing is stored; the dots are never sent, and a field holding only dots, of any number, counts as not typed in. The eye that shows what is typed is there only while the person edits the field, in every password field.** Why: the owner, "I do not like \"(unchanged)\"", and an eye that cannot reveal anything should not be there ("The eye should ONLY be visible when editing").
