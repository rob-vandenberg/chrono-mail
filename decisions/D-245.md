# Nothing sent the person did not send
Topics: [[pop3-smtp-sending]] · [[storage]]

(the Windows computer's W6, the owner) **Nothing is sent that the person did not send, schedule or let a refresh send.** Nothing checks by itself after a restore, an index rebuild or an import ([[D-300]]). A restore brings the Outbox's mail back in Drafts and pauses a scheduled message whose time passed. Why: a restore of an old backup sent its old Outbox.
