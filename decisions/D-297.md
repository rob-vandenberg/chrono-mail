# Timed events in colour in Month
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(UR-02, the owner) **Month can show an account's timed events in their colour**: the tick "Fill colour for timed events" in a calendar's right-click menu, off by default, kept with the account (this computer's view only). It is in the menu only while Month is shown, the only view it changes (the owner's choice over OR-15, 2026-11-11). On, a timed event in Month is drawn as an all-day one. Why: the dot was too small to tell events apart.
