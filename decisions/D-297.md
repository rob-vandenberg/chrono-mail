# Timed events in colour in Month
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(UR-02, the owner) **Month can show an account's timed events in their colour**: the tick "Show timed events in colour in Month" in a calendar's right-click menu, off by default, kept with the account (this computer's view only). On, a timed event in Month is drawn as an all-day one. Why: the dot was too small to tell events apart. (Until 4.4.573 the tick was in Event colours, gone with [[D-329]].)
