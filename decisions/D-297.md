# Timed events in colour in Month
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(UR-02, the owner) **Month can show an account's timed events in their colour**: Event colours has the tick "Show background colour for timed events in Month view", off by default, kept with the account's colours. On, a timed event in Month is drawn as an all-day one. Why: the dot was too small to tell events apart.
