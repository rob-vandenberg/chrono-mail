# Show where a link leads
Topics: [[security]] · [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **Where a link leads is shown before it is clicked**: the full address in the bottom bar while hovering; an orange triangle when it leads to another main domain than the sender's (Public Suffix List, never fetched), a mailto link judged by every address it writes to, also after its ?, and never on one that writes only to the addresses of the person's accounts (4.3.503; never the To line, which the sender writes). Why: a quoted own signature in nearly every reply made the triangle meaningless.
