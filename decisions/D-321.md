# The day headings follow the day
Topics: [[mail-window]] · [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(the owner) **After midnight the message list looks exactly as after closing and reopening the program**: the day headings ("Today", "Yesterday", "Earlier this week" …) are those of the new day and none is folded; the selection, the reading pane and the place of the message at the top of the screen stay, and nothing else changes: the person did nothing. The engine counts the headings at its own clock and says the day they were counted for; the list asks them again in place when the day changes (a look just after midnight, at most every five minutes, and when the program becomes active again), and a failure stays silent until the next look. Why: a list left open from Sunday to Tuesday showed Monday's mail under Sunday's "Today" (a person's report).
