# Everything of one tag in one overview
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **"Show everything tagged…" in a tag's right-click menu** (Tags in the folder list, the Tags lists of Contacts, Tasks and Calendar) opens a window of its own for that tag: one list with four foldable groups and their counts (Contacts; Appointments to come, the earlier ones folded; Tasks open, the finished ones folded; Mail newest first, loaded as you scroll), a reading pane, a search field for all four; a double click opens an item as its section does. It follows every change in place and only shows: nothing is changed, no mail is marked read. Why: a person wanted to see everything of one customer together; the reference client has no such view, Chrono Mail adds it.
