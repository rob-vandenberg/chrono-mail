# The reference client's signatures and templates come into the library
Topics: [[importer]] · [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **The import from the reference client brings every signature and template of its settings export into the library of [[D-358]], and each account here of an address they are used for gets them as its choice** for new mail, replies and forwards, with the signature's place. A name already in the library is skipped and its text left as it is (never a second name, never overwritten); the account then gets the text of that name. A choice the account already has stays. A text is its name and HTML only: a signature's pictures are inside it already, a template's pictures (attachments the text names) are put inside it; a template's subject, recipients, font and attached files are not kept, the files named. A signature and a template of one name: the second is skipped and lost, its accounts get the first. The export's "Sent from" tick switches on Chrono Mail's own. The import window has its own tick "Templates and signatures" after Rules (on at the start); without a settings export it brings nothing. Why: an import brings everything as it is ([[D-124]]) and must never change or double what the person has.
