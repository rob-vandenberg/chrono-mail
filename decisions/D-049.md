# From the server to here
Topics: [[imap]]

Server to here, per folder: EXAMINE, UIDVALIDITY, removals, flags, new mail; a message enters the state only after it is stored and verified. Removals are refused when more than half of at least 10 vanish. A UIDVALIDITY change matches by Message-ID; unmatched mail goes to `Recovered/<folder>`. Roles from SPECIAL-USE, then the usual names.
