# Standard folders known by role
Topics: [[imap]]

(CR-36, the owner) **A standard folder is known by its role, never by its name, and is never deleted**: every folder with a role (Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Trash, Junk, Archive, and Chrono Mail's own Outbox, Scheduled, Snoozed, Recovered), also inside a folder being deleted. Delete folder… and Rename folder… are greyed for it; a rename the server made is always followed. Why: a guard of six English names let "Sent Items" or Archive be deleted on the server with its mail.
