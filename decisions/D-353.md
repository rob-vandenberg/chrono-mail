# Pictures inside mail
Topics: [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **A picture can stand in the text of a message**: dragged in, pasted, or chosen with Picture… in the formatting bar; a dropped file that is not a picture is attached, never its path typed. In plain text a dropped file and a pasted picture become attachments, nothing typed (the owner: "once converted to plain text, the user can add images, but only as an attachment, not inline"). It is sent **unaltered, at its own size**, as an inline part the HTML shows by cid:; the editor shows a copy no wider than the text. Drafts, Take back from the Outbox or Scheduled, and IMAP drafts keep the pictures; going to plain text makes them attachments, except in a reply: a reply in plain text has no pictures at all, the person's own neither, also as a draft opened again (the owner: "A reply converted to plain text loses its images"). A reply or forward in rich text quotes the original with its formatting and pictures; a plain reply has neither its pictures nor its attachments; a plain forward carries its pictures and attachments as attachments; nothing is written to disk unencrypted. Why: a mail program must send pictures, and the reference client and Gmail change only how a picture is shown, never the picture.
