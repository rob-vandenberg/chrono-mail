# Tags give appointments their colour
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[tags]]

(the owner) **An appointment has no colour of its own: its tags give it.** In the appointment window each tag is a rounded label in its colour with a radio button; the checked one is the colour tag (the first added keeps it, a click moves it, removing it gives it to the first one left; kept as the first of its tags, so it reaches the server and every computer). Every tag shows as a small tag in its colour at the end of the appointment's line; hovering shows title, times, calendar and tags. Without tags: another program's colour, else the calendar's. Existing colours became tags of the same name and colour. Replaces [[D-191]]. Why: two ways to colour an appointment confused people, and tags also filter ([[D-327]]) and gather ([[D-328]]).
