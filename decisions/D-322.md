# The calendar and the tasks follow the day
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(the owner) **After midnight the calendar and the task list look exactly as after closing and reopening the program**: today is the new day in every calendar view, a calendar that showed today shows the new today (a date the person went to stays), and the tasks are grouped as Overdue, Today, Tomorrow from the new day, with their overdue marks; the selection and the scroll place stay. The same day watch as the message list ([[D-321]]) looks just after midnight, at most every five minutes and when the program becomes active; a failure stays silent until the next look. Why: the calendar and the tasks kept the old day as today until something else made them load again.
