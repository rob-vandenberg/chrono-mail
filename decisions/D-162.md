# How the decisions are kept
Topics: [[way-of-working]]

**Every decision is one short file in `decisions/`, its full wording in `decision-details/`, and DECISIONS.md is the entry point** with the core decisions and the topics. The short version says what is decided and why, in two or three sentences: enough to apply the decision without opening its file; most fit in about three lines, a longer one is the exception and needs a reason. Why: every chat reads DECISIONS.md at the start, so it must stay small, and nothing may be lost.
