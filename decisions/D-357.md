# A private handover between chats
Topics: [[way-of-working]]

(the owner) **`HANDOVER.md` carries one chat's work to the next**: the last chat's versions, the next work in order, the open problems and what to watch out for; at most about one page, replaced (never appended to) when the owner says "handover", private and git-ignored. A new chat reads it right after STATUS.md and starts at its Next work. Why: PROMPT-NEW-SESSION.md is the same every time, so the next work was lost between chats; STATUS.md is too long for it and is public.
