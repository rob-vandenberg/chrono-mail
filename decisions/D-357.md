# A private handover between chats
Topics: [[way-of-working]]

(the owner) **`HANDOVER.md` carries one chat's work to the next**: the last chat's versions, the next work in order, the open problems and what to watch out for; replaced (never appended to) at the end of each chat, private and git-ignored. It is kept current during the chat: every decision about the next work goes into its Next work at that moment, in the owner's quoted words. At "handover" it is checked and completed, and its Next work 1 is shown to the owner with his quoted words for his yes before the chat ends; a new chat reads it right after STATUS.md and first names Next work 1 in one line and waits for the owner's yes. Why: a handover rebuilt from memory at the end of a long chat twice sent the next chat to the wrong work.
