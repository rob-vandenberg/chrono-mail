# Unverified certificate asks at once
Topics: [[security]] · [[imap]] · [[pop3-smtp-sending]]

(UR-05, the owner) **A certificate that cannot be verified asks at once**: the Verify certificate window opens by itself, one at a time, each server once. Connect trusts exactly this certificate until Chrono Mail closes, or for good with Don't ask me again; a changed certificate asks again. TLS without a certificate check is the person's own choice, never the program's (OR-6). Why: a click on a button first was not what anyone would guess.
