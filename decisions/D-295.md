# A column sorts by what it shows
Topics: [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **A column sorts by exactly what it shows, and a sort belongs to the column it was chosen by**: no two columns share a sort, so changing one column never moves the sort to another. From / To sorts and groups by exactly the person it shows: in Sent, Drafts, the Outbox and Scheduled its first recipient, else the sender. Why: "Sorting on a column sorts on that column. Period."
