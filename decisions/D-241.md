# Delaying a send
Topics: [[pop3-smtp-sending]]

(B-21, B-20, the owner) **Delaying a send**, two independent ticks in Settings > General: a delay of N seconds (the message waits in the Outbox with a "not before" time; a notice at the bottom left offers Send now and Cancel), and "until the next refresh". Every send of the Outbox keeps the "not before" time; one message at a time is sent, never twice. Quitting with mail in the Outbox asks first, with Do not warn again.
