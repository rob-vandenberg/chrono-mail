# The colours a person makes in Google Calendar are tags here
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[tags]] · [[basic-rule]]

(the owner) **A colour the person made in Google Calendar and gave a name, a label, is a tag of that name and colour here**, on the events that carry it and in the tag list; a label without a name gives its event that colour only. Give an event a tag of a label's name here and Google gets that label, on Google first ([[D-193]]); without such a label it stays the nearest of Google's eleven colours ([[D-337]]). Google carries none of this over CalDAV: its Calendar API answers a calendar's labels and an event's label only when the request says `eventLabelVersion=1` (seen live). Why: the person's own named colours are what they see on Google's website, and a number out of eleven is not one of them.
