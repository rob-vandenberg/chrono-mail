# Series exceptions follow a new time
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the re-check of the review, RV-185 to RV-188) **A whole series changed: its skipped and changed dates follow only a new time of day.** When the rule and the first day stay, each moves on the wall clock by as much as the start; when the first day or the rule changes, they stay as they were. Rejected: finding each date again by its place in the rule (fragile against hostile rules; dates could clash).
