# Include in Send and Receive
Topics: [[imap]] · [[pop3-smtp-sending]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the owner) **Every account has the tick "Include this account in Send and Receive"** (Edit account > General, on by default). Unticked it takes part in nothing: not the refresh at start, Automatic refresh, the new-mail signal, Send and Receive All, Receive All, Send All (its Outbox waits) or the calendar sync; only its own entry in the Refresh menu reaches it. What the person sends by hand still goes. Why: the owner asked for this tick. Leaving a folder out → [[D-120]].
