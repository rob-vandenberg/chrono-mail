# An account and a calendar are named by what the person and Google know
Topics: [[look]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[pop3-smtp-sending]]

(the owner) **Settings > Accounts shows each account on two lines**: its Shown as name with its address ("Rob <rob@example.com>", or the address alone), and below it, smaller and grey, what it holds ("Mail (POP3)", "Google Calendar", "Google Contacts", "Calendars and contacts"); the server only when two accounts are otherwise the same. **Chrono Mail never makes up a Shown as name**: an account without one is named by the person's name on Google (read again at every synchronisation, so a change there shows here), else its address; **a calendar named only by an address** is shown by the name of the account with that address, else of the contact with it, else the address. Why: "the representation is nice, not confusing, unambiguous", and a made-up name went stale and said nothing.
