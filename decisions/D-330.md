# A separator above the Tags list, its place kept per section
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[tags]] · [[look]]

(the owner) **In Calendar, Tasks and Contacts a separator to drag sits between the folder list and the Tags list; each section keeps its own place through a restart.** The first time it is 3 to 2, as before; a hidden Tags list ([[D-327]]) hides the separator and keeps the place for when it comes back. Why: a fixed split left a long list of calendars or tags cut off with no way to make room.
