# Two Google colours of one name are one tag of one colour
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[basic-rule]]

(the owner) **Within one Google calendar, two colours so close that they get the same name are one tag of one colour**, the last one read deciding, and the appointments of both are drawn in it; a name that stands for two colours is the person's to settle in Google. The earlier rule that Google's colour also overwrote the person's own tag of that name is replaced by [[D-341]]: a Google colour is a tag of its calendar and never touches a tag the person made. Why: Google holds the colours, so a name there is one tag here.
