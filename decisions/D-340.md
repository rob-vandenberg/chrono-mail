# What is in Google counts: its colour wins on a tag of its name
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[basic-rule]]

(the owner) **A tag whose name is a colour of one of the account's Google calendars always has that colour**, whoever made the tag, so a colour changed in Google always arrives here ([[D-193]], [[D-339]]). A colour the person gives such a tag is replaced at the next synchronisation; two Google colours so close that they get the same name are one tag of one colour, the last one read deciding, and the appointments of both are drawn in it. A name that stands for two colours is the person's to settle, in Google. Why: Google holds the colours, so a name there is a name here; anything else means a change in Google never reaches the person.
