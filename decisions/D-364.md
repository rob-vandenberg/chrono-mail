# Each Google account keeps the client codes of the person's own Google project
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[security]]

(the owner) **The owner's Google codes are never shipped; the person types the client ID and client secret of their own Google project when adding Google Calendar and Contacts (Sign in with Google is greyed until both are there), and can change them on the account's General tab.** Each Google account keeps its own: the client ID with its settings, the client secret in the credential store; a change is saved for every Google account of the same Google address, which share one sign-in. Why: anyone could take shipped codes and use the owner's project, which Google could then block for every user ("That risk i want to avoid, indeed!"); per account, "but we cannot be sure of that so we do not guess".
