# Readable wallet entries
Topics: [[changes-reach-the-screen]] · [[security]]

(B-23, the owner) **Every entry of Chrono Mail in the wallet carries a text a person can read**: "Chrono Mail: <account name>, <address> (incoming mail server)" (outgoing, calendar and contacts server), "Chrono Mail: the key that encrypts your mail". A text that cannot be written fails nothing. Why: the lookup names told the person nothing.
