# The same tabs on Settings > Sidebar and Settings > Tags
Topics: [[look]] · [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the owner) **Settings > Sidebar and Settings > Tags have the same tabs: Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Tasks, the sections' names and order, each tab as wide as its name from the left.** Settings > Sidebar's Contacts tab works as its Calendar and Tasks tabs: the address books shown in the sidebar (the last one cannot be left out; the one shown when it is left out gives way to the first) and the Tags tick ([[D-327]]); a contact in a left-out address book opens from Mail's Contact icon in its own window. Why: two pages that do the same had different looks and names, and Contacts was missing.
