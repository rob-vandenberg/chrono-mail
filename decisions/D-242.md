# Documentation in three layers
Topics: [[way-of-working]]

(the owner) **Documentation in three layers, each for one reader**: `README.md` for the people who use the program, a user guide in `docs/` with one page per thing a person wants to do, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` for developers. Why: a big program needs a front door that answers "what is it, how do I get it" in 30 seconds.
