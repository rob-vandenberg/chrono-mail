# Moving mail between accounts
Topics: [[pop3-smtp-sending]] · [[imap]] · [[sync-conflicts]]

(the owner) **Messages move to a folder of another account message by message**: each is read with its marks and tags, stored in the other account and read back, and only then removed from its own account for good. Stopped at any moment, a message is in one account or both, never in neither; done again, nothing is stored twice. A refused upload leaves the message where it was and says why; refused later after an offline move it is a sync conflict ([[D-195]]).
