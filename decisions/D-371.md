# Dragging an appointment
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(B-36, the owner) **An appointment is dragged to another time or day, or by its end to another length, inside the view it is in**: the hours with their all-day row (Day, Work Week, Week, Upcoming), and Month and n weeks; not Agenda. It lands on the quarter of an hour or the day where it is dropped and keeps its length; a timed one dropped in the all-day row becomes all day, an all-day one dropped on an hour lasts an hour. What Edit cannot change cannot be dragged; a repeating one asks this date or all, your meeting whether the attendees hear of it; it shows at its new place with the clock until the server answers, a refusal puts it back ([[D-193]], [[D-325]]); Ctrl+Z takes it back ([[D-368]]). Why: what every person expects of a calendar.
