# Undo and Redo in Calendar, Tasks and Contacts
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(B-18 step 3, the owner) **Ctrl+Z undoes what the person expects it to undo; Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z does it again.** Calendar, Tasks and Contacts each keep their own list, so the key acts on the section that is shown. Undone are an edit (in the window, a title typed in place, tags, Mark as done), a delete (also of one date of a series), a move, Cancel and Restore, an answer to an invitation, and a contact group renamed, deleted or its members changed; making something new is not undone (the person deletes it). An undo is a new action, server first ([[D-193]]); an item changed since is left alone and told; nobody is mailed: what was sent stays sent. What came back is selected ([[D-325]]). Why: the owner, "If the user did X, and then pressed Ctrl+Z, what would he expect to happen? Besides those things beyond our control, we do what we can to undo what he wants undone."
