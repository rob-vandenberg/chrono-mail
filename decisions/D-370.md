# Google Tasks in the Tasks section
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[security]]

(B-14, the owner) **A Google account's task lists are in the Tasks section like any task list, through Google's Tasks API** (Google keeps no tasks in its CalDAV). New, edit, done, delete, move and Undo go to Google first ([[D-193]]); a task changed both here and on Google is a sync conflict, never an overwrite. Google keeps a title, notes, a due date (no time), done or not, and subtasks; a due time, priority, start, tags, reminders and repeats stay on this computer ([[D-201]]). Every done task shows as done, also one Google hides; only a task Google deleted leaves the list (the owner, Q138). The sign-in also asks for the tasks permission (never mail, [[D-335]]); a Google account signed in before, or a Google project without the Tasks API, is told in plain words what to do, and its calendars and contacts go on.
