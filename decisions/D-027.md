# Own POP3 client, delete last
Topics: [[pop3-smtp-sending]]

Own POP3 client with caps (line 1 MiB, message 256 MiB, 1,000,000 messages, timeouts); STLS never skipped. Order: retrieve, store and verify, log with fsync, only then DELE (committed by QUIT). Deletion is blocked when the store looks damaged or empty, or more than half of at least 10 are missing.
