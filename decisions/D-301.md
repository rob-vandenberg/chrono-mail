# Imported calendar accounts
Topics: [[importer]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(UR-01, the owner) **An imported calendar account is given to the mail account with its address** when that one has no calendar server yet; else it becomes a Calendars and contacts account. The source's separate CalDAV and CardDAV accounts of one server address and login are one here ([[D-185]]). Nothing is asked of the server at the import.
