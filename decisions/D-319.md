# D-319 A click in the Flag or Unread column toggles that mark
Topics: [[changes-reach-the-screen]] · [[mail-window]]

(The owner) A click in the Flag or Unread column of the message list turns that mark on or off, on that one message; the selection stays, as with the Contact and Delete icons. With Remove messages from Unread when I mark them as read, the click takes the message out of Unread; when it was not selected the selection stays as it is, and with nothing selected nothing gets selected and nothing opens. The cells have no hover text, because many hovers quickly annoy; the headers say "Flagged" and "Unread". Why: the marks are in view, so the person expects a click on them to work.
