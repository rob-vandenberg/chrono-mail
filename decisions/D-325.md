# An appointment stays selected and shows its change at once
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(the owner) **An appointment is known by itself, not by what it holds**: after any change (in place, in the appointment window, from the server) it stays selected and its block stays on the screen until its new version replaces it; only a delete or a move to another or an unticked calendar takes it away at once. **A title typed in place shows at once with the clock** until the server answers: its yes takes the clock away, its no brings the old title back and the status line says why; while the clock shows, the title cannot be edited again. Why: never leave the person in the dark, and never make them select again what they just changed.
