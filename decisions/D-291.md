# From and To shown three ways
Topics: [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **From and To are one column each, shown one of three ways**: Name and email, Name only (no name: the address), Email only (no address: the name). Another way takes the column's place and width; a sort of From or To always follows the way shown. Rejected: a tick per way, each a column of its own (three From columns was possible and confusing).
