# Marked-mail views read only marked mail
Topics: [[storage]] · [[mail-window]]

(the owner's report) **A view of marked mail reads only its marked mail, whatever the sort** (Unread, Flagged, a tag). A change of the read mark costs a little more. Why: these views opened every message of every folder to test its mark, 14 to 31 s on a spinning disk the first time.
