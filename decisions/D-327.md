# A tag filter in every section
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the owner) **The Tasks and Calendar sidebars have a Tags list, as Contacts has**: "All tasks" or "All events", then every tag in use there with its count; a click on a tag shows only its items (in Calendar in every view), "All …" shows everything again. The list follows every change in place; when the chosen tag is left with no items, everything shows again. It only filters, nothing changes here or on the server. Settings > Sidebar's Calendar and Tasks tabs have a Tags tick that hides the list (and then filters nothing). Why: a person asked how to see everything of one tag; Mail and Contacts could, Tasks and Calendar could not.
