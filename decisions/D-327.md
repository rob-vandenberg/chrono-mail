# A tag filter in every section
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the owner) **The Tasks and Calendar sidebars have a Tags list, as Contacts has**: every tag in use there with its count, drawn as a folder's unread; Mail's Tags likewise lists only the tags some mail carries, with their number; a click on a tag shows only its items (in Calendar in every view), a second click everything again ([[D-334]]: no "All …" line, the active tag stays while active). The list follows every change in place. It only filters, nothing changes here or on the server. Settings > Sidebar's Calendar, Contacts and Tasks tabs have a Tags tick that hides the list (and then filters nothing, [[D-331]]). Why: a person asked how to see everything of one tag; Mail and Contacts could, Tasks and Calendar could not.
