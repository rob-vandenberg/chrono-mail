# D-317 Cancel and Restore of a calendar event
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(The owner) Cancel sets the status Cancelled, server first, on the whole event or one occurrence: it stays, grey and inert (D-316). An event of your own mails nobody. Your own meeting with attendees: the same, plus the mail dialog and a real cancellation, for the whole event or that occurrence; on Kerio Chrono Mail mails it, as Kerio says nothing of a status. An invited event: your own copy only, same ID, no mail; the organizer's next version replaces it. Cancel of a whole series also covers a changed date with no status of its own; a date with its own status keeps it.

(The owner) Restore only undoes Cancelled, on the whole event or the occurrence, and leaves no status, whatever it had before. On your own meeting with attendees it sends a new version and they answer again. On an invited event it writes your own copy only, no mail, also when the organizer cancelled it; it stays active until the organizer's next version.

(The owner) On a Cancelled event every action that changes a property is greyed until Restore: the four answers, Edit, opening it; only Restore and Delete stay, as Delete acts on the entire event at once. Cancel is greyed when Cancelled or hidden, Restore unless Cancelled. The commands refuse the same (OR-4). Answer and status remain two kinds of state. Delete of a Cancelled meeting never asks; nothing remembers whether a mail went (D-310).

How it is built (not the owner's words): `event.cancel` and `event.restore`; `event.edit` no longer sets Cancelled, which is what mailed "Changed". Don't send keeps the version and the attendees' answers, as nobody is told. You keep your own answer on your own meeting at Restore. A newer version from the organizer can be answered on a Cancelled event, as it replaces the copy. A date restored in a Cancelled series has no status and shows active, so a changed date goes by what it says itself, also in `cancelled_on` and `cancelled_in`. The scope question for Cancel has Close, not a button Cancel. The mail of Restore is "Restored: title", "title is on again". The details file has why.
