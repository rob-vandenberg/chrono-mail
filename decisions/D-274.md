# Each profile has its own wallet name
Topics: [[changes-reach-the-screen]] · [[security]]

(the owner) **A profile keeps its passwords and key in the wallet under a name of its own**, "chrono-mail (<folder name>, <code of its place>)", never with a "/"; the person's own keep "chrono-mail". Why: the demo's made-up passwords were mixed with the person's real ones; KDE's wallet garbles an entry of a name with "/" when its label changes (B-23).
