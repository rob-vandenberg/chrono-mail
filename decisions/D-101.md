# Archive and Unarchive
Topics: [[mail-window]]

Archiving notes the source folder; Unarchive goes back there (else the Inbox). **Archive is Unarchive when every selected message is in an archive folder**, wherever they are shown (a folder, Favorites, a search of all folders); a mixed selection archives the others. It follows the messages, never the folder on the left. Ctrl+J is Junk / Not junk.
