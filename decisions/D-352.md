# Settings > Calendar and the n weeks view
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[look]]

(the owner) **Settings > Calendar** has: Show week numbers, Display shorter events the same size as 30 minute events, Reduce the brightness of past events (all off), Start week on (System default, Saturday, Sunday, Monday; for every view and every date picker), Set custom view (2 to 6 weeks, 4) and Number of days in Upcoming (2 to 14, 7). The custom view shows n weeks with the week of today in the top row, unlike Month; Upcoming and the custom view start at today when chosen and the arrows move them by their days or weeks. Kept on this computer only (view settings, no server data). Why: the owner's way of planning, as the big web calendars offer it; Show weekends is the Work Week view, Show completed tasks waits until Calendar shows tasks.
