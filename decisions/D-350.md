# What is deleted is erased after 30 days
Topics: [[storage]]

(the owner) **Deleted calendar, contact and task items and deleted mail folders are erased from the disk 30 days after their deletion**, by a command at every start (`pim.purge-deleted`), in the background. Nothing in the program can bring them back from `.deleted`, so keeping them for good only filled the disk. Why: [[D-072]] promised 30 days, but nothing erased them.
