# Renaming a Google calendar gives it a name only for you
Topics: [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[basic-rule]] · [[tags]]

(the owner) **Rename of a Google calendar sets your own name for it on Google** (its entry in your calendar list), server first, never the calendar's own name: a CalDAV rename changes that for everyone who sees the calendar (seen live), so Esther's shared calendar would lose her name for her. An empty name gives Google's name back. Google's CalDAV gives your own name as the calendar's name (seen live), so the sidebar and the tags ("Rood (Esther)") follow it at every Refresh, also on your other computers. Why: Google names a main calendar after its address, and its website's "Esther Van Vliet" does not reach its CalDAV or Calendar API.
