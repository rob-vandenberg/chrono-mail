# One name per meaning in the account windows
Topics: [[pop3-smtp-sending]] · [[look]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]]

(the owner) **Four names, used the same way in every window:** Shown as (the label Chrono Mail shows for an account; only you see it), Your name (goes out with your mail), E-mail address (the address on your mail) and Sign-in name (what you sign in to the server with). "Account name", "User name" and "Login name" are gone. A calendar account shows what it signs in with on its General tab, read-only ("Google account" for a Google one), and a Google account says "Signed in with Google as …" without sign-in name and password fields. Why: "Account name" sounded like the account itself (the address), and a Google account showed nowhere which Google account it was.
