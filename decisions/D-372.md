# Dragging a folder
Topics: [[mail-window]] · [[imap]]

(B-38, the owner) **In Mail's folder list a folder the person made is dragged onto another folder of its account, or onto its account, and moves there with everything inside it.** Standard folders never move ([[D-269]]); there is no move to another account. The folder that would take it gets the blue bar; a drop into itself, where it is already, where a folder of its name is, or into Chrono Mail's own folders is refused. It is a rename to the new path: server first, offline here at once and there at the next check, a lasting refusal a sync conflict ([[D-193]]); the rules follow it ([[D-302]]); Ctrl+Z moves it back ("Move folder", [[D-369]]). Why: what every person expects of a folder list.
