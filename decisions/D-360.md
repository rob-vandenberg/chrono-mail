# A reply never changes the original's style; own text is one line high
Topics: [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **A reply or forward quotes the original with every style it came with, and the original's style rules reach the quote only, never the text the person writes.** The person's own lines (the "On … wrote:" line, a forward's header lines, every line typed) are one line high with no space above or below a paragraph: one Enter gives the next line, two Enters an empty line, in the editor and in the mail that is sent. Why: the person answers; only the answer may change, never what is answered, and space nobody typed is not wanted (the owner: "If i want an extra empty line i will hit an extra Enter").
