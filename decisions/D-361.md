# The office suite's signatures come into Templates and Signatures as they are
Topics: [[importer]] · [[mail-window]]

(the owner) **The import from the office suite's mail program brings every signature of its signatures folder into Settings > Templates and Signatures as it is**: its HTML unchanged, its pictures put inside it, its name as there. A name already there is skipped and left as it is ([[D-359]]); a signature without its HTML file is left out and named. Each account here gets its New Signature for new mail and its Reply-Forward Signature for replies and forwards, unless it has one chosen already. Why: an import brings everything as it is (OR-22, the owner: "IT READS AND WRITES. THAT IS WHAT AN IMPORTED DOES!").
