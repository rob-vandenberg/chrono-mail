# Local actions take rows out at once
Topics: [[changes-reach-the-screen]]

(the owner) **An action that needs no server's word (POP3, Local Folders) takes its rows out at the key press**; the action waits in turn behind the earlier ones and runs in the background. A failure brings its messages back at their places and says so in the status line. IMAP waits for the server ([[D-193]]). Why: Delete must be instant, also with the key held.
