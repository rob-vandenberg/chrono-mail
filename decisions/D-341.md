# A Google colour is a tag of its own calendar
Topics: [[tags]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[basic-rule]]

(the owner) **A tag the person makes is known by its name; a tag that comes from a Google calendar by its name, a vertical bar and the calendar's Google id** ("Rood|ann@gmail.com"), so the colours of two calendars, or of two accounts, that share a name are two tags, each in its own colour, and never touch a tag of that name the person made. On screen such a tag is only ever "Rood (Esther)", the calendar's name in brackets; the bar and the id never show, and no name the person types may hold a bar. Calendar tags are not in Settings > Tags: they are renamed and recoloured in Google Calendar and followed at the next Refresh ([[D-193]]); in the appointment window Add tag… offers that calendar's tags, and one chosen goes to Google first. Why: "She has hers, I have mine": one tag list for the whole program let another calendar's colour overwrite one's own.
