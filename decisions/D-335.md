# Gmail: mail with an app password, calendar and contacts through Google sign-in
Topics: [[imap]] · [[calendars-contacts-tasks]] · [[security]]

(the owner) **Gmail mail is an ordinary IMAP account logged in with an app password** (2-Step Verification on): Add account offers "Gmail", which fills in imap.gmail.com 993 and smtp.gmail.com 465 (TLS) and greys them; a refused login says what an app password is and where to make one. Gmail's All Mail starts hidden (it holds every message once more); Delete moves to Gmail's Trash and Archive to a visible Archive folder (a Gmail label), so Ctrl+Z keeps working. **Google Calendar and Google Contacts use Google sign-in** (OAuth with PKCE) through the owner's own Google project "Chrono Mail" (In production without Google's check since 2026-11-13: no time limit on the sign-in, at most 100 users), never the mail scope (only the address and name, calendars and contacts), so no yearly security review is needed; its client codes stay in a private file outside the project. Why: Google refuses plain passwords since 2025, and its mail scope requires a paid yearly assessment that freeware cannot carry.
