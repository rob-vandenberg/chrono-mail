# Each size slider changes its own part and nothing else
Topics: [[look]]

(the owner) **Settings > Appearance > Sizes has seven sliders, each strictly confined to its own part. Main window: every part of the main window, its menus and its tooltips (the top bar, the sidebar, the status line, the reading pane's subject, sender, picture, attachments and invitation card), except: Message list (the row with Sorted by, Filter and the count, and the list), Calendar, Contacts and Tasks (everything right of the sidebar and below the top bar of that view) and Text of messages (the content of a message, wherever it is shown). Other windows and dialogs: every window and dialog that is not the main window, with its menus and tooltips; a menu is not a window and takes the size of the window it opens from ([[D-135]]). At the update the Calendar, Contacts and Tasks sliders start where the Main window slider stands.** Why: when a person scales a part, every element of it scales the same way and nothing else changes; the content of a message has a slider of its own because the sender styled it, everything else Chrono Mail styles itself.
