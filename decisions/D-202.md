# Follow the server's subscriptions
Topics: [[imap]]

(R-34, the owner) **The server's subscriptions are followed; the ticks in Edit account > Folders are this computer's only** (never subscribe or unsubscribe). A folder taken off the list elsewhere is synced one last time, then its copy here goes; one put on it comes. Standard folders are always synced; a folder with something the server has not got stays until sent or settled.
