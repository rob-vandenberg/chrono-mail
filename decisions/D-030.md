# Safe HTML, engine answers, three panes
Topics: [[security]] · [[engine]] · [[mail-window]]

(security) HTML mail without JavaScript, plug-ins or storage; no outside request until allowed per message; links only after a question; subjects and headers never markup. (engine) `Backend` runs commands on a thread pool and delivers answers on the GUI thread; every job answers exactly once however it ends. (mail window) Three panes; Delete moves to Trash (in Trash it asks). Refresh is Send and Receive (per account too, [[D-059]]); Settings > General chooses what runs at start and on the timer (the owner). Nothing else sends or receives by itself ([[D-300]]).
