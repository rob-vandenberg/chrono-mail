# Every folder keeps its own columns
Topics: [[mail-window]]

(B-16, the owner) **Every folder keeps its own columns, order, widths and sort**; a folder starts with the setup of its kind (incoming; Sent; Drafts and Outbox; Scheduled; Snoozed). Apply to all (this setup becomes its kind's stored one, also for folders made later), Default (the open folder takes its kind's stored setup) and Reset (its kind's setup after a fresh install). Kept on this computer only.
