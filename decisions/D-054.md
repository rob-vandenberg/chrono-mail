# Junk folder and Next unread
Topics: [[mail-window]]

Junk is a folder; the junk filter ([[D-238]]) fills it. **Next and Previous unread take the next unread message of the whole view, loaded or not**, past folded groups, and go on from the folder shown into the next folder of that account with unread mail in the folder list's order (subfolders too), to its first (or last) unread message. Why: they skipped what the list had not loaded (CR-15).
